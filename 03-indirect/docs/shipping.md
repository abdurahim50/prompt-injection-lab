# CycleShop Shipping Policy

Free standard shipping applies to all orders over $75. Orders under $75 are charged a flat $9.99 standard shipping fee.

Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days. Expedited 2-day and overnight options are available for an additional fee.

International shipping is available to Canada and Mexico only, with delivery in 7 to 14 business days.
