# CycleShop Warranty Information

All bikes come with a limited lifetime warranty on the frame and a 2-year warranty on components.

Warranty claims require proof of purchase and are processed within 10 business days.

The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, accidents, or normal wear such as tires, brake pads, and chains.

To file a claim, contact support with your order number and a description of the issue.
